Space Weather Official Risk Pipeline

A lightweight dashboard for modelling simplified LEO satellite drag stress under changing atmospheric conditions.

Pipeline

Loads satellite data, models atmospheric density, propagates orbits and scores stress risk.

Risk Levels

Classifies satellites into low, medium and high drag-stress categories.

Parallel Processing

Uses async orchestration with process-based parallel propagation across satellites.
